Learned the hard way

Four things that cost me generations

Helmets and suits swap clean. Faces don't.
My first cast was horror villains. Freddy and Michael came out looking like guys in Halloween costumes. Characters defined by a full suit or helmet, like Vader and stormtroopers, come out looking like the real thing.

Your main dancer is the strongest swap.
Put yourself on the person closest to the camera. Background swaps get softer and smaller, so give them simple, iconic looks.

Say "empty hands" out loud.
If the reference photo has a lightsaber or blaster, the AI brings it. Write "hands empty, no weapon" next to that character.

Tag every character every time.
Write the @Image tag next to each name, with a plain space on each side. Untagged names get guessed.
